Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Bg Free is a background-removal tool that runs entirely in your browser. This page explains exactly what that means for your data, in plain terms, and covers every category of data this site touches.

Your photos never leave your device

Bg Free does not have a server that receives your images. When you upload a photo, it is processed on-device using a machine learning model that runs locally in your browser via WebAssembly. At no point is the image you're editing transmitted to us, stored by us, or seen by us. This applies to every image operation in the tool, including the Magic Brush, background replacement, and background blur — all of it happens locally.

What is downloaded to your browser

The first time you use Bg Free, your browser downloads the background-removal model (roughly 45–90 MB) from a third-party content delivery network so it can run locally. This is a one-time download of our model files — it does not involve sending any of your photos anywhere. Standard web request metadata (such as your IP address) is visible to that CDN in the same way it would be for any file downloaded from any website, since that's how HTTP requests work generally.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymized usage of this site — for example, how many people visit, which pages are viewed, and general device/browser information. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to do this. It does not have access to the photos you process, since those never leave your device. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using browser extensions such as the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by blocking cookies for this site in your browser settings.

Advertising

This site does not currently show advertisements. We have applied for Google AdSense, and while that application is under review, this page includes AdSense's standard site-verification script — this does not serve ads and does not change what happens to your photos. If the application is approved and ads are turned on, AdSense would use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, which may include personalized advertising based on your visits to this and other sites. You would be able to control ad personalization through Google's Ads Settings, and this policy will be updated — with the "last updated" date above changing accordingly — before any ads go live. Enabling AdSense would never change how your photos are processed; that part of the architecture (fully on-device) would remain the same.

If you're visiting from the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you may see a consent banner asking you to accept or decline non-essential cookies before continuing to use the site. This banner is served through Google's certified consent management platform as part of our AdSense setup — it controls cookie-based ad personalization only, and has no effect on how your photo is processed, since that happens locally regardless of your choice.

Local storage

Bg Free uses your browser's local storage to remember small preferences on your own device — such as your light/dark theme choice and whether you've dismissed the announcement banner. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

No accounts, no signup

Bg Free does not have user accounts, logins, or a database of users. We don't collect names, emails, or passwords, because there's nowhere for you to enter them.

Your choices

  • Block or clear cookies for this site at any time in your browser settings — the tool itself still works, since it doesn't depend on cookies to process images.
  • Use the Google Analytics opt-out extension linked above to stop analytics tracking entirely.
  • Clear your browser's local storage for this site to reset saved preferences (theme, banner dismissal).

Children's privacy

Bg Free does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the tool doesn't collect personal information from anyone in the first place, this applies equally to all users.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly.
